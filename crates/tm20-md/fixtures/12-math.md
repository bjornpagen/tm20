# Maxwell

In spacetime algebra the Faraday field is one bivector \(F=\mathbf{E}+Ic\mathbf{B}\), or \(\frac{1}{2}F_{\mu\nu}\gamma^{\mu}\wedge\gamma^{\nu}\). Gauss, Faraday, Ampère, and Maxwell collapse to a single grade equation on the tape.

\[
\nabla F = \mu_{0} c J
\]

Expand in the Pauli algebra and the grades split: \(\nabla\cdot F\) is Gauss–Ampère, \(\nabla\wedge F\) is Faraday. The source is \(J=c\rho-\mathbf{J}\).

\[
(\partial_{t}+\boldsymbol{\nabla})(\mathbf{E}+I\mathbf{B})=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_{0}}-I\frac{\mathbf{J}}{\epsilon_{0}c}
\]
